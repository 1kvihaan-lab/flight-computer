# October 1–31: Designed the flight computer PCB

Spent the month designing the PCB for my high-power rocket flight computer. 
Started by selecting the electronic components, then created the circuit schematic in KiCad.
After completing the schematic, placed the components onto the PCB layout and routed the traces 
to connect everything. Ran into some issues with the solder pads but managed to figure them out.
